For businesses

Hundreds of small amounts. One organized recovery.

Unclaimed funds for a business rarely sit in one account. They're often scattered across hundreds of small items reported over many years: refunds, credits, uncashed checks, and overpayments, listed under misspellings and variations of your name, former business names, and old addresses. Many never show up in a simple search.

My database tools and matching process are built for exactly this. I find the items, verify which belong to you, and prepare and file every claim and supporting document. Your part is reviewing and signing.

Common questions

Straight answers before you decide.

Is this legitimate?

Yes. I'm licensed as a Claimant Designated Representative in Georgia (License No. 202400072) and registered as an unclaimed funds investigator in California, and I never ask for any payment upfront.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. My fee is set out in a written agreement before any work begins and is paid only if your funds are recovered.

What will you need from us?

Mainly documentation proving your organization's identity and its connection to the funds, along with a signed agreement. I'll walk you through exactly what's needed once we get started.

How long does it take?

Once the required documents are completed and the claim is filed, it typically takes about five months until the state sends payment. Funds are paid directly to you by the state — I never handle or hold your money.

Will I need to meet with you in person?

No. Once you're ready to sign, I arrange for a mobile notary to come to you at a time that works for you, at no cost to you. I'll walk you through exactly what you're signing beforehand, so the signing itself is quick — typically 15 to 20 minutes.
